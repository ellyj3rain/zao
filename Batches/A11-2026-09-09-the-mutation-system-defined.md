## Record

`ROADMAP.md` has said *draft owed* against the mutation-axes fork since
`[A2]`. `MUTATION.md` is the answer, and it is a definition rather than
a draft: the operator defined the system directly and this records what
they said.

## How it was arrived at, because the method was the hard part

Three attempts failed first, and each failed differently.

**A menu, before there was a system.** Options were put to the operator
about where mutation attaches to the infection course. There was nothing
to attach it to - the question presumed an architecture nobody had
described.

**Authorship, after being told the menu was wrong.** A draft of four
continuous axes was begun unilaterally. That is the opposite error and a
worse one: substituting a design for the operator's, in a project whose
whole rule is that intent is theirs.

**A local fix, after being told authorship was wrong.** DR-001 uses the
word *decay* for both of its mechanisms - the mind losing what it held,
and what the pathogen does to tissue - and the response was to propose
separating those two senses. Still bottom-up. A colliding term is a part,
and parts cannot be defined before the whole they belong to.

The operator's correction names the posture: take what they set, carry
it forward far enough that they can see it was understood, map it, and
then surface the decisions that fall out of **their** frame. Act on it
and loop them in where it makes sense, rather than deferring at every
step or building past them.

**And the writing was refused once on its own terms.** A map written in
invented vocabulary - trunk, exits, breadth, degree - was unreadable, and
said so. What replaced it says the same things in ordinary words with
concrete cases.

## What the definition contains

The system is in `MUTATION.md`. In outline:

Infection is a repeated event; surviving enough produces resistance and
then immunity, both counts dials. Every infection rolls the same odds
across a gradient of mutations, with crossed rarer and carrying its own
number, and every infection also carries a rare variable of its own that
makes a body sicker faster and takes it *past* death rather than to it.

The gradient holds two kinds of thing: mutations that give a body
something it can do, built from models already held, and mutations that
only make a body more of what it was. Most wait for death and some do
not.

The two ends are opposites in one respect - **how much of a person is
left** - and appearance is independent of it. **Crossed** looks like a
person and is not one: cognition intact, every capability intact, every
drive intact, humanity burned out. **Afflicted** looks like a monster and
is a person again: lessened, sometimes catching, unable to be a person
among survivors because of what survivors see. An afflicted body cannot
become one of the dead, and is likelier than anyone to become crossed,
which is what it fears most.

Capability at both ends comes from what remains of the person, which is
DR-002 carried past the turn.

Houses decide about their own and can split over it. The afflicted are
driven to gather and driven toward places survivors avoid, and **success
is never encoded** - a shanty town is an outcome or it does not exist,
which is the sister's DR-037.

The crossed organise, act with a person's vocabulary - driving,
explosives, loudspeakers - ignore the dead by default and use them, and
work on the afflicted deliberately. That last needs no new machinery:
torment raises fear, fear taxes moodles, and moodles already feed
susceptibility in the infection model this project absorbed.

Identity decays alongside the physiology, episodically rather than
steadily, with which axis it takes varying per body.

The player walks the same road and can become either.

## Two consequences worth naming separately

**ZAO owns both.** Everything past infection belongs to the project that
owns the pathogen, including the ones who came back.

**Which opens a second seam.** `PROJECTS.md` lists one crossing between
the projects - the turn. There is now another, running the other way: a
person becomes this project's while staying inside the sister's county,
in a house, holding bonds, being argued over. The sister's machinery for
houses, standing, fear and temperament reaches a body this project owns,
and `PROJECTS.md` does not describe that yet.

**And the crossed need the sister's action machinery**, stripped of what
humanity gave it. That is a larger claim on the sister than anything
this project has needed before, and it is not designed here.

## No new border

A definition is not behaviour and the tree still has no mod code. Border
1 gained a row: `MUTATION.md` is a root document, so doc currency holds
its header and `MEMORY.md` classifies it.

## What is still the operator's

Strain names. How many mutations sit on the gradient and what each is.
Every number behind every dial. How an afflicted settlement is held once
one forms. Whether the crossed hold ground the way a house does. The
fork ledger keeps them.
