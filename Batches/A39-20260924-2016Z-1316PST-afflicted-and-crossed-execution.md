## One owner, distinct lives

ZAO now executes both living states created by its pathogen. An authorized
Afflicted return transfers the same human shell directly into `ZAO.Driver`.
An Afflicted-to-Crossed conversion changes the policy selected by that driver
without replacing its durable token. SAO remains the owner of county identity,
communication, source use, handover and locomotion services; a ZAO person uses
those services without gaining a second SAO controller.

The common driver contains only common execution mechanics: current activity,
capability projection, arbitration, durable routes, native work admission and
exact-once route results. Afflicted policy reacts to observed
Crossed threats, seeks actual Afflicted peers and travels toward evidenced
ground. Crossed policy retains hunting, intentional exposure, driving, holding
and its own material actions. Afflicted require water, prefer meat/protein, can
use non-dairy alternatives with reduced relief and a temporary penalty, and may
individually choose human-origin food. Crossed policy separately admits human
wound care, drinking, rest and food under reduced caloric pressure.

Settlement formation now requires actual state-specific holding activity from
three distinct people over multiple days. A repeated scan of one body and
co-location without a holding act cannot form a group. Formation and need
evidence persist with the group; an unrepresented member's last observation is
not silently rewritten as health or idleness.

## Crossed food and blood are material actions

Crossed retain human physiology and ordinary food can satisfy hunger. Human-
origin food remains preferred where sustenance and altered motives reinforce
one another. A timed, interruptible butchery action
accepts an ordinary or Afflicted human corpse; Afflicted sources are
dispreferred, while Crossed, mutant, zombie and animal sources remain excluded.
Completion produces bounded human-flesh items exactly once. The flesh
is cookable and participates in native evolved recipes, including stew; the
diet reader admits a resulting dish only when its extra-item ledger retains the
human-flesh provenance. Stopped or reconstructed actions produce nothing.

A separately chosen timed action prepares the exact equipped melee or projectile weapon
with Crossed blood. The mark holds a finite use count. A hit is journalled,
then resolved after the native attack has had an opportunity to create a wound.
An eligible ordinary living human takes the engine's native Knox-infection path
on an existing wound. An Afflicted target goes through ZAO's already separate
intentional-exposure receipt and ownership path. Crossed, mutant and dead
targets are refused. Reload interrupts unresolved preparation and hits rather
than inventing contact.

These are concrete first producers, not an exhaustive Crossed vocabulary.
Weapons, stew and butchery do not become a feature list that defines the
person; blood preparation is not automatic before predation. Later verbs
require their own current capability, material and engine owners. Crossed
strategy is retained human cognition, not a rare subtype or nearest-target
loop; this batch proves only a bounded tactical option set.

## Distinct maintenance and predatory results

`ZAO_Maintenance` advances both living states across loaded and dormant time
without applying survivor auto-food. Protein clears the Afflicted alternative
penalty. A completed human meal can temporarily reduce later intentional
Crossed-exposure risk according to the donor's living health and Knox
adaptation. Crossed predatory pressure remains separate from hunger and changes
only through an exact completed result. `ZAO_Predation` requires heard speech
plus public flight for fear, native health loss for pain and a post-threat
completed yield for control; death gives none of those results.

Personal food acquisition calls SAO SourceUse with a registered Diet adapter.
SAO still owns private source revision, locomotion, exact transfer, carrying,
native measurement and the terminal receipt. Diet owns only item admission and
state-specific consequence. Active source work survives the ZAO driver's
serialization and can be interrupted by acute state pressure.
