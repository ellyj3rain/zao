## One process owner, distinct reasons to act

The shared `ZAO.Driver` now lets each state policy originate, revise, deliver
and withdraw a durable matter through SAO Organization and Communication. It
does not contain a second social planner. Afflicted and Crossed supply different
private evidence, options and appraisals; the driver supplies only the common
execution boundary and keeps the same process across later state change.

Afflicted can raise provisioning from actual personal hunger or thirst, or from
an evidenced settlement necessity. The proposal names food or water and actual
known recipients. Fatigue, injury or a state label cannot manufacture caloric
need. When that necessity resolves, the originator withdraws the open matter.
Crossed can instead propose a bounded rendezvous and holding act to up to three
currently evidenced associates. Retained home, held ground or present
opportunity supplies the destination; discipline, initiative, relationships,
current activity and pressure affect whether each person accepts, qualifies,
counters, declines, defers, contests or later withdraws.

Recipients appraise only after actual acquisition and from their current
ZAO-owned state. Missing representation or execution evidence produces defer,
not inferred incapacity. Conversion does not recreate the process: the exact
proposal revision survives while the same person reappraises through the new
policy. Thus an Afflicted acceptance can become a Crossed qualification when
the person's current interests and pressure change, without leaking the
condition label into the generic decision record.

Accepted work returns through SAO's existing native owners. Food and portable
stored water use SourceUse, carrying, Locomotion and Handover. Rendezvous uses
native Locomotion and completes only on the exact promised arrival activity.
The driver's current activity records actual completed holding; that evidence
can later participate in ZAO's already established settlement formation.
Interruption, partial/failure results, reload reconstruction and exact-once
terminal consumption remain owned by the sister's process and service stores.
