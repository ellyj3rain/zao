## Intentional exposure

Normal Crossed states now reach the existing decision pass even though their
form is `none`. When the selected target is Afflicted, the controller routes
the body through a distinct blood-exposure action. The carrier approaches,
holds contact for 0.025 county hours and clears its ordinary attack target.
Range loss, target loss, ownership changes or an unavailable body interrupt and
archive the action. Afflicted people cannot fall through to feeding behavior.

The action journal authorizes the pathogen result. `Pathogen.expose` accepts
only a completed result whose carrier, target, kind, token and resolving phase
match the live durable action. Repeating a token returns its prior receipt;
proximity and forged results cannot roll. The existing probability remains
Crossed odds multiplied by Afflicted susceptibility. There is no spontaneous
Afflicted conversion.

## Crossed body ownership

On a successful result, ZAO takes the same living human shell from SAO instead
of changing terminal state beside an SAO controller. The supported person
snapshot, appearance, inventory and exact identity remain attached to the one
record. ZAO controls loaded shells, hibernates them outside the control band and
materializes them through the external-owner token. A busy target retains a
durable pending marker; after save/reload ZAO can claim the checkpointed dormant
envelope directly. A dead shell is never driven again and returns through SAO's
death and corpse funnel. Result and transfer journals retry and archive
independently, so a committed conversion cannot be rolled twice.

ZAO also owns the versioned brain-health table when installed. SAO supplies
physical observations and effects; pathogen transitions supply terminal state
at the exact event hour. Standalone SAO retains its own copy of the same schema.
