## The rulings

**The principle first, because it decides the rest.** The operator's
frame, stated as the project's definition: three repositories, one
project, separation of concerns. Each machinery runs what it owns.

**SAO executes the afflicted.** A person again is a person: the four
pillars run them, the house argues over them because they never left
the county's machinery, and their bonds and records keep their
provenance. This project owns the pathogen state on an afflicted body -
the susceptibility to crossed, the passive infection, the
identity-decay dials. The state is the boundary between the two, and
the claim surface lives on it.

**The claim surface question dissolved rather than answered.** Put
concretely, the operator said N/A - and the controller ruling explains
why: the surface is what the state boundary is, and which machinery
runs the body decides what has to cross it. The concrete shape stays
with F-012 until mod code is near, where the ledger already notes it is
cheap only while none exists.

**This project executes the crossed.** They came through death as the
risen did, and exactly one controller runs any body.

**The crossed's vocabulary is a bidirectional goal.** The direction is
ratified: read the sister's action machinery, stripped of what
humanity gave it, copying no files - the turn's pattern extended from
the record to the living vocabulary. And the sister has not mapped
driving yet, which makes the seam run two ways in time: what the
crossed need from driving, explosives and loudspeakers feeds forward
into the sister's mapping of them, and neither side invents its half
alone. A standing goal between the two repositories, not a dependency
in either direction.

**How the crossed hold ground is variable, and the variability is the
design.** They still enjoy things and have leisure, differently. Their
shifted needs reshape how they live in a space they have decided is
valuable to them. Some groups settle; some stay nomadic. Which one a
crossed group does is what its drives did, never a placement - DR-037's
law, applied to them as to every arrangement in the county.
