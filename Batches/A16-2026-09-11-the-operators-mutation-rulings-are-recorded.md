## Record

On 2026-09-11 the operator answered seven questions on the mutation
system in the project-surface thread `zao-mechanics-decisions-2026-09-11`.
This batch carries those rulings into the living records. The remaining
six questions were withdrawn by the caller after the operator ruled that
several of the questions carried ungrounded presuppositions that did not
match the direction already set; the seven answers stand.

## The rulings

- **The mutants source port supplies the forms of the gradient (DR-017).**
  The mutants mod's forms and animations are raw material. Attributes and
  forms are distinct but linked: a bruiser can look the part and be the
  part, and attributes and animations can also be conferred
  non-physiognomically. A mutant-form body can come back as one of the
  afflicted, and a returned mutant diversifies how the afflicted present
  and the capabilities they carry forward.
- **The gradient's contents are enumerated from what is possible
  (DR-018).** What sits on the gradient is enumerated from what can be
  made possible, using the ported forms and the suggested concepts as the
  basis for variety among the known types and for expanding beyond them.
- **The dial numbers extrapolate from the operator's suggested numbers
  (DR-019).** The defaults come from numbers the operator has already
  suggested. Extracting those numbers into the tree is named work; no
  dial value is authored by the assistant.
- **Afflicted memory is fractured at the turn, not necessarily decaying
  after it (DR-020).** The turning and the return already fracture and
  disorganize memory. Not every afflicted body loses more over time;
  where further loss occurs it is episodic, governed by the existing
  dials and genuinely random beyond them.
- **Necessity holds an afflicted settlement (DR-021).** What holds a
  cast-out settlement together is necessity, and the mechanics of holding
  follow from what the members need. Success stays unencoded.
- **Nothing decides the house split in advance.** The question's
  presupposition was rejected: the house decision can go either way and
  is an emergent outcome, which the records already state. No new rule
  was needed; the rejection is recorded here.
- **The rarity question combined two areas of concern.** The rarity fork
  is split into the spread's tails and settlement frequency, both open.
