## State-owned elapsed restoration

`ZAO_ExecutionOwner.beginDormancy` admits only the exact published ZAO person,
body and owner token. `ZAO_Maintenance.beginDormancy` captures a bounded detached
copy of its own maintenance field. SAO's body restoration continues elapsed
physiology through the existing registered ZAO owner while replaying its own
native pharmacology. If restoration fails, `rollbackDormancy` restores only
that captured maintenance field, once. Successful restoration drops the
transient snapshot. The transaction creates no durable body reference or
second state owner.

The paired C89 Body controls preserve the original foreign release journal
across incomplete removal and reload, validate it before native removal, refuse
a replaced owner, and allocate no shell when replay ownership is unavailable.
Failed native restoration retires or retains its exact partial shell for
teardown; a failed rollback keeps an explicit unknown-state boundary.
