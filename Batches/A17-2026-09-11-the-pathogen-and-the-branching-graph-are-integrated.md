## Record

The operator answered six integration questions in the Mousecat thread
`zao-branching-integration-rewritten-2026-09-12`. Several of the questions
restated decisions already carried by the living records. This batch records
the rulings that were still open and closes the duplicate surface.

## The rulings

- **The pathogen owns the mutation roll.** The living branching graph does
  not choose a form. The default roll is uniform across the gradient, and
  sandbox settings may weight it.
- **The pathogen also owns the roll for form performance.**
- **Crossed is a terminal pathogen state.** A crossed body does not mutate
  further and does not organize around forms. A crossed body may
  instrumentalize or value a unique form in another body.
- **Retained form traits are state, not new branches.** They change what a
  body is and what pressure it carries; they do not open a second branch
  after the first has been taken.
- **Capability forms and attribute mutations stack.**
- **Forms are visible facts.** They enter Perception and change pressure
  inside the living branching graph.
